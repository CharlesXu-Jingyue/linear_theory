One set of symbols runs through all thirteen chapters. Each chapter still defines every symbol where it first appears; this page collects them, flags the few deliberate collisions, and spells out every abbreviation.

Typesetting conventions

  • Vectors are columns; $x^\T y$ is a scalar and $xy^\T$ a matrix.
  • Displayed equations are followed by a "where" list defining each new symbol.
  • Definitions, theorems, propositions, lemmas, corollaries, examples and counterexamples share one counter per chapter (Theorem 7.4 follows Definition 7.3). Problems, probes, patterns, conjectures and remarks carry run-in labels without numbers.
  • Every general claim is the statement of a numbered block (a Theorem, Proposition, Lemma or Corollary, or a Counterexample when something fails), followed by its proof, a proof sketch or a precise source. Heuristics appear only inside Remarks and are marked as such.
  • Frequency responses are written $G(i\omega)$ with $i^2 = -1$.
  • In live readouts and figure labels, symbols are set in plain italic type and subscripts with Unicode digits; everywhere else math is typeset.
